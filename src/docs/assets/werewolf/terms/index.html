Werewolf Terms of Use

Last updated 22 November 2026

These terms cover the Werewolf party game provided by stemosoft. They apply whether you play as a guest or sign in with Google or Apple.

Joining a game

Only join rooms you have been invited to. Share the room code or QR code with the people you intend to play with. Choose a table name you are comfortable showing to everyone in the room.

Playing with others

Bluffing about your role is part of Werewolf. Treat the people at the table with respect. These rules apply to table names, clues and any other content you share through the game.

  • Do not threaten, bully, harass or impersonate another person.
  • Do not share hateful, sexually explicit, exploitative or illegal content, or content that infringes another person’s rights.
  • Keep other people’s personal information private unless you have their permission to share it.
  • Do not access someone else’s account, private cards or room without permission, or deliberately disrupt the service.

Reporting a problem

To report abusive content or conduct, email stemosoft@gmail.com. Include the table name involved, the approximate time and a short description so we can identify the incident. Keep passwords and sign-in codes private. We may remove content or restrict access in response to misuse.

Your account

You can stop playing at any time. To request deletion of your Werewolf account and its associated data, email stemosoft support. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting on the request.

App-store terms and your rights

The applicable store terms also govern your download. For the App Store version, see Apple’s standard software licence. These terms preserve the consumer rights you have under applicable law.
